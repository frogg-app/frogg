Round 3 — one language, six IDE layouts

Back on the IDE side: standard places for everything, clear labels, nothing to learn. The look comes from the frogg.dev site: near-black with grain, cyan into deep blue, faceted shards, cut (chamfered) corners instead of rounded pills, and slow, smooth ease-out motion. Instead of an accent bar on the active row, the selection glides between rows (a plate, corner brackets, a notch, a slanted highlight). Status is a shape plus a word: ▲ running, ◆ needs you, ⬢ done, ⚑ failed, ◇ idle. Same fake data and behaviour as before. Round 2 · Round 1

Remix: developer-first mixes

Six mixes built from the parts above, with function first: an attention-sorted inbox with inline approve (A), a fleet bar, session tabs (1–9), a terminal panel (`), a Changes / Terminal / CI pane (\), a status bar showing what needs you, ⌘K, and j/k everywhere. Branding stays light but present: project shards, the frogg mark in the rail and status bar, cut corners, cyan, and gliding selection.

General notes (cross-direction: mix-and-match, things to keep or kill)

Tips: click around the session list quickly to see the selection glide · j/k moves · hover rows for the cursor spotlight · Facet: Changes/Terminal/PR tabs · Bracket: “Changes” slides a drawer open, A approves · Slate: close and reopen tabs, collapse folders and the bottom panel · Prism: the left rail switches project scope, Chat/Changes/Terminal toggle.
